# Collab cups

The 2018 tie-in tournaments of the Japanese release are back as the International Tournament:
the FINAL FANTASY 30th Anniversary Cup, two LEVEL-5 20th Anniversary Cups and the Famitsu Collab
Cup. Each cup has a qualifier and a final, and the prizes are the tie-in outfits.

## Turn them on

In the launcher, open the Saves tab and set Collab outfits and cups:

| Setting | Cups | Outfits in the shop's coin tab |
|---|---|---|
| Classic cups | Qualifier cut +3, unlimited rounds | 500 coins a part |
| Hardcore cups | Qualifier cut -3, finals on champion tees in strong wind, 3 rounds per stage a day | 1,500 to 2,500 coins a part |

In both modes the outfits are not handed out at the start. Win them in the cups or buy them in
the shop. The other settings (All unlocked, Earn in game, Original) run no cups.

## Play a cup

1. Press Play. With the Online tab left empty, the launcher starts a small server that only your
   PC can reach. No router port is opened and no invite code is shown.
2. In the game, open the menu and pick the International Tournament. Play the qualifier.
3. When the round ends, open Notices. The qualifier results notice gives the entry prize.
4. If your score made the cut, the International Tournament now shows the final. Play it, then
   open its notice in Notices for the final prize.
5. The next cup then opens. After the last one, the list starts over.

All of this works in one sitting. The game's calendar moves on with your rounds, so there are no
dates to wait for. Missed the cut? Play the qualifier again.

| Cup | Qualifier prize | Final prize |
|---|---|---|
| FINAL FANTASY 30th Anniversary | Cactuar outfit and the twelve Cactuar caps | Moogle suit |
| LEVEL-5 20th Anniversary 1 | Toan and Xiao outfits | White Knight outfit |
| LEVEL-5 20th Anniversary 2 | Evan, Shaati and Layton outfits | Jibanyan and Komasan suits |
| Famitsu Collab | Famitsu T-shirts and caps | 3,000 coins |

The Famitsu final gave ranger suits in 2018. Their textures are not in the game files, so the
final pays coins instead. Evan's pants and three of the Famitsu T-shirts also have no model or
texture in the game files.

## Hosting and joining

When you host, your server runs the cups for you. When you join a friend's game, the cups come
from the host's server and follow the host's Collab setting. A cup on a shared server follows one
player's progress, so the cups are meant for one player at a time.
